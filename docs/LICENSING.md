# Licensing

## Project license

NØVA Flash Studio is licensed under **MIT OR Apache-2.0** — your choice. The
authoritative declaration is `license = "MIT OR Apache-2.0"` in the
`[workspace.package]` section of `Cargo.toml`.

Full license texts:

- `legal/LICENSE-MIT.txt`
- `legal/LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt`

Both are also shipped inside every distribution package
(`licenses/` folder of the portable bundle and of installed copies).

## No activation, no account, no telemetry

NØVA Flash Studio is **free to use without product keys, license keys or
activation codes**. It does not contact a license server, does not require
registration or an account, performs no periodic license validation, and is
fully usable **offline** after installation. No telemetry or analytics are
sent to the publisher or third parties.

## Third-party components

The application links numerous third-party Rust crates. Their license
expressions are inventoried in `legal/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`, generated by:

```bash
python scripts/gen-third-party-notices.py
```

Re-run that script after changing dependencies. The SPDX license texts for
identifiers found in `LICENSES/` are shipped under `licenses/` in every
package. `LICENSES/` texts can be refreshed with
`python scripts/fetch-license-texts.py` (downloads official SPDX texts).
